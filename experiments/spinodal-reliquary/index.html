What is actually evolving?

A conservative Cahn–Hilliard mixture coarsens into connected domains. Nutrient deposits mineral at their interfaces. Mineral slows both phase motion and nutrient transport. Accumulated deposition survives dissolution and sets the optical film thickness.

This is a dimensionless 2D model, rendered as a perforated 2.5D relief. Its wet lighting and three-wavelength interference are artistic approximations. No fluid mechanics or full spectral transport is claimed.

The mixture is warm-started before mineralization. Dark openings correspond to the other phase; this is evolving implicit connectivity, without mesh remeshing.
